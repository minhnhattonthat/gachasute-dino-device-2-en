## 4. Odd, debug or unused text

- **Debug and test strings (bank 00):** 0000 and 0020 digit strings, plus 0069 "0123456789%9". There are also a page-switch test (0001), a two-choice test (0002-0004), a number-display test (0005), a string-display test (0006), a device-name display test (0007) and "-DEBUG DATA- OAM= CNT= R= L= B=" (0008-0009). These are not shown in normal play. Translate them cheaply or leave them.
- **Empty entries:** about 1090 of the 2031 entries are only `[close]`, empty, or control codes with no text. Examples: 000A-000F, 0033-003F, 0071-007F, 029A-029D, 0412.
- **Placeholders:** 0045 "−−−−−−−−" is the blank name and 006A "−" is a single dash. 0081 is all spaces and blanks a line. Keep their widths.
- **Already English (fullwidth):** 0086-008B "DINO CHARGE", "Setup Dinomo Entry 1st/2nd/3rd", "OK", "Good Luck!!". Only convert them to the halfwidth font.
- **Other-version string:** 0083 フェニックスバージョン "Phoenix Version" is probably never shown in Dragon. Translate it anyway.
- **Anti-tamper / corrupt-RAM warning:** 0216 says "illegal processing occurred, RAM contents abnormal, contact User Support in the manual". It ends mid-sentence ("なお ゲームはこのままつづけられますが" = "the game can continue as is, but"). It is only shown on a bad save or cheats.
- **Typo in the source:** 0126 てにいてた should be てにいれた.
- **Dialect win lines:** 0222 is a random pick (E0 34) from two sets. 0223-0227 are standard Misari lines. 0228-022C are in regional dialects: 0228-0229 Tohoku-ish (〜さねぇ, つよいなや), 022A Hakata (バリつよ, すごかね), 022B-022C Kansai (めっちゃ, 〜やわ, うち, おめでとな). These may be joke or random variants. Render them as playful accents (e.g. Southern/cowboy, "well I'll be"), or flag for the user.
- **Gante's name is inconsistent:** ゼペットガンテ (025F) vs ゼニーノ・ダウ・ガンテ (0377). The user should decide.
- **Duplicated scenes with small variants:** 0128/0261 and 012F/0261 (store closing; one route skips the sale wait). 0148/0149 (cafe). 014B/0234 (Dino Breaker aftermath: one version after a loss, one after a win). 0207/0208 are identical. 01F0-01F4 are near-identical "bag full" scenes. 0341/0351/0361/0371 are tournament-loss copies.
- **Orphan fragment:** 0144 "いえ ないといえば ないような・・・" has no speaker context. It is probably a reply in a choice branch.
- **Deliberately garbled overheard speech:** 01A1 "だか・・・・だろ", "・・・せー・・・よ・・・ら〜". Keep it fragmentary in English.
- **Puns needing localization:**
  - 044A: the hero almost says ば(くだん) "bomb" and Misari covers with "バリバリ めしくいて〜" (he always says he wants to chow down). Use a "B-" word gag, e.g. "B... burgers!".
  - 0378: オーバーテクノロジー misheard as オーバーテイク/オーバーコート, then "うまいのか？" ("is it tasty?").
  - 0148: the waitress gets every order wrong. Keep the food list.
- **Pre-battle taunts with no portrait:** 0700-0717 are opponents' lines (070B is Kabira's style, 0717 a timid girl). The speaker is chosen by code.
- **Credits:** 0800-0804 are hiragana staff names. Render them in Western order with Hepburn: Katsutoshi Fujioka, Hideki Takeshige, Manabu Watanabe, Toshiya Matsuyama, Tatsuya Akaishi, Yoka Studio (?), Keisuke Abe, Keita Komiyama, Ayano Saito, Shinobu Matsuzawa, Takako Nakamura, Yuki Miura, Kennosuke Suemura, Ryo Kasai, Atsuya Matsuo, Studio PJ, Satomi Hoshino, Koichi Kobayashi, Hikaru Horiuchi. "だいな ジロー" is a joke pseudonym, "Jiro Dino" (?).
- **Number and name inserts:** `[E0 11 nn]`/`[E0 17 nn]` numbers produce the "０ビット" seen in 0157/0400; the 0 is a placeholder digit. `[E0 18]` is the tournament opponent's name (0500-0503) and `[E0 19]` a town name (0131). Word order in EN must keep the inserts working.
- **Name tables:** the Dino, Device, person and place names are in `script/misc.json`, not in the banks (see section 2, Dino names). This pass only skimmed them.
